About Our Class

We are Information Technology students interested in programming, web development, computer networks, databases, and Technological innovation.

Mission

To learn, collaborate, and develop skills to create Technological solutions that are useful to society.

Vision

To build a participatory, creative class that is prepared for the challenges of the information Technology market.

Areas of Interest

Web Programming, Python, Java, databases, networking, and cybersecurity.

Technology transforms ideias into solutions. We learn together to turn knoledge into impact.
